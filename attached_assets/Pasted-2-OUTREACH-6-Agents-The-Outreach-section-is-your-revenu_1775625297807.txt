2. OUTREACH (6 Agents)
The Outreach section is your revenue engine starting point. It has 6 tabs, each powered by a dedicated AI agent. The flow goes: Find Prospects (Agent 1) > Plan Approach (Agent 3) > Draft Messages (Agent 4) > Send & Follow-up (Agent 5) > Analytics (Agent 6). The Social Command Center (Agent 2) monitors all incoming messages across channels.

2.1 — Prospect Finder — "Find Prospects" button

HOW IT WORKS:
This is where it all starts. Click "Find Prospects" and optionally enter search criteria (industry focus, location, company size). Agent 1 (Prospect Intelligence) researches cybersecurity and IT companies, analyzes their marketing weaknesses (bad website, no social presence, weak SEO), verifies contact info (email, LinkedIn), and scores each prospect on Fit (0-100, how well they match your ideal client) and Accessibility (0-100, how easy to reach decision makers). Returns a ranked list of prospect cards.

IN AI AUTONOMOUS MODE:
Button visible and prominent. AI runs immediately. Results appear within seconds. No approval needed. Each prospect card auto-shows action buttons.

IN HYBRID MODE:
Button visible. AI runs when you click. Results appear with "AI Generated" badge in corner. You review the list before taking action on any prospect.

IN HUMAN CONTROLLED MODE:
Button may be hidden or relabeled to "Research Company" (manual). You enter company details yourself. AI may assist with scoring only if you request it.

EXPECTED RESULT:
