PART 31 — PERMISSION MODEL

The platform must enforce four primary permission levels:

Super Admin
Admin
Manager
User

These roles must shape actual system behavior, not merely hide buttons. Permissioning must control:

page visibility
module visibility
action rights
approval rights
publishing rights
financial access
archive access
CRM visibility
reporting visibility
quality visibility
system controls
integrations
governance settings

The permissions model must be tied to workflow authority and business risk. It should govern who may generate, approve, publish, view, override, escalate, archive, and administratively control records or actions.

A mature operating system cannot rely on cosmetic role distinctions. Permissions must influence how the platform behaves.

PART 35 — FINAL CREATION MANDATE

This is a new system creation from zero. That principle must remain active through the full lifecycle of design, architecture, build, and rollout. Nothing foundational may be treated as optional if it belongs in the operating system core.

Accordingly:

no major layer may remain shallow
no domain may remain cosmetic
no reporting system may be an afterthought
no archive layer may be missing
no permission model may be weak
no AI layer may be generic
no workflow may be disconnected
no business-critical function may be postponed if it belongs in the foundation

The product to be built is:

a real AI-powered company inside software

It is not:

a prototype
a dashboard
a loose collection of tools
a partially intelligent application
a stitched-together internal utility

It must be a complete business operating system worthy of real operational dependency.
