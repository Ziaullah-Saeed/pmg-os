PART 18 — CRM, SALES, CLOSING & REVENUE PIPELINE DOMAIN

This domain must be among the strongest operational layers in the platform because it governs revenue movement. It must understand sales not merely as data entry, but as progression through stages of commercial momentum, trust formation, decision support, and opportunity conversion.

It must support:

lead acceptance
opportunity creation
deal progression
stage transitions
activity logging
follow-ups
meeting records
proposal status
contract status
owner assignment
handoff tracking
stale opportunity detection
close probability awareness
post-close transition into delivery

This domain must understand revenue flow, not just contact management. It should preserve context from prospecting through qualification, outreach, meetings, proposals, negotiations, close, and transition.

The CRM layer must help the company answer:

Where is revenue likely to come from?
What deals are healthy?
What deals are stalled?
What action is needed to move a deal?
What activities matter most right now?
Where is follow-up missing?
Which opportunities are becoming stale?
Which handoffs are incomplete?

This domain must support operational discipline and leadership visibility simultaneously.

PART 20 — COMMUNICATION INTELLIGENCE, CALLING & MEETING SUPPORT DOMAIN

This domain must go far beyond a standard phone dialer or meeting notes tool. It must function as a communication intelligence environment capable of improving how PMG prepares for, conducts, and follows up on sales conversations and meetings.

It must support three distinct operating modes:

Mode A — AI-Led Calling

Where AI supports or manages calls within defined boundaries, including:

call eligibility logic
qualification logic
conversation flow logic
objection handling within safe limits
booking logic
logging
disposition mapping
CRM updates
next-step generation
Mode B — Human-Led Cold Calling with AI Guidance

Where the human remains the active caller, while AI supplies:

company context
lead context
likely pain summary
objection map
opening suggestions
tone guidance
next-best phrase suggestions
meeting ask timing guidance
exit guidance
objection-response options
Mode C — Human-Led Zoom / Meeting / Closing Support

Where the human remains the closer or meeting lead, but AI supports through:

transcription
objection detection
interest signal detection
hesitation detection
need analysis
answer suggestions
emphasis suggestions
next-step recommendations
follow-up draft generation
meeting summary generation
CRM update assistance

This domain must integrate tightly with:

CRM
booking logic
reporting
archive
follow-up workflows
revenue movement

Communication is one of the highest-leverage moments in the system. This domain must therefore improve preparation, confidence, relevance, follow-through, and conversion quality.

PART 21 — HUMAN + AI COMMUNICATION COORDINATION

This domain must be governed by a central principle: AI exists to amplify human effectiveness, not merely replace voice actions. The purpose of communication intelligence is not to strip out the human role where trust and judgment matter. It is to make the human operator sharper, faster, more aware, and better supported.

Accordingly:

AI-led calls are one operating mode
AI-guided human calls are another
AI-guided human meetings and closes are another

The system must improve:

confidence
consistency
objection handling
situational awareness
preparation quality
communication follow-through
post-meeting execution discipline

This domain should feel like a world-class strategist beside the operator. It should reduce improvisational weakness, lost context, poor note retention, and missed opportunities for better responses.

At the same time, it must preserve human authenticity. Communication support must not turn meetings into robotic scripts. It must enhance human judgment while preserving natural, trust-building interaction.

PART 22 — FINANCE DOMAIN

The finance domain must be robust enough to support real operational use. It must not be symbolic. It must enable visibility into revenue movement, billing, payment status, cost awareness, and financial risk.

It must support:

quotations
estimates
invoices
receipts
payment records
outstanding balance tracking
expense logs
profitability snapshots
cost tracking
usage billing awareness
time-based billing support
tax-ready export support
internal revenue visibility
client financial state visibility

The finance layer must answer:

What was billed?
What was paid?
What remains outstanding?
What is profitable?
What is costing money?
What is becoming financially risky?
What needs follow-up?

This domain must integrate meaningfully with CRM, delivery, reporting, and archive functions. Financial data should not live in a disconnected corner. It must be part of the company’s actual operating visibility.
