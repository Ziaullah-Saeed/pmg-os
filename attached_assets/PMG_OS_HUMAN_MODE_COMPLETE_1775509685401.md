## SECTION 1.1: UNDERSTANDING HUMAN MODE

**What is Human Mode?**

Your team does EVERYTHING manually. No AI. Pure human work:
- Team researches prospects
- Team writes content
- Team qualifies leads
- Team creates proposals
- Team generates reports

**Why Human Mode?**
- Complete control
- Highest quality
- Personal touch
- Deep customization
- Team learning
- Relationship building

**Challenges:**
- Takes much longer
- Requires skilled team
- More expensive
- Less scalable
- Manual tracking needed
- Quality depends on person

## SECTION 2.1: FIND PROSPECTS (Manual)

### Process: Manual Prospect Research

**Time Required:** 10-15 minutes per prospect = 3-4 hours for 20 prospects

**Tools Needed:**
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- Apollo.io or Hunter.io
- Google Sheets
- Notepad

### Step-by-Step Process:

**Step 1: Define Target (30 minutes)**

Create a profile of who you want:

```
TARGET PROSPECT PROFILE

Industry: Cybersecurity, IT Security, Cloud Services
Company Size: 50-1000 employees
Revenue: $5M - $50M
Location: USA/Canada
Pain Point: Not generating enough qualified leads
Decision Maker: CISO, VP Security, VP Sales

Company Characteristics:
- Growing (40%+ YoY growth)
- Well-funded
- B2B/SaaS
- Tech-savvy leadership
```

**Step 2: Search on LinkedIn (4 hours for 20 prospects)**

1. Open LinkedIn Sales Navigator
2. Use filters:
   - Industry: [your target]
   - Company size: [your range]
   - Location: [your target]
   - Keywords: [lead gen, sales, marketing]

3. For each company found:
   - Note the company name
   - Find the CISO or VP Security
   - Write down their name, title, email
   - Research company on their website
   - Note company size, funding, growth
   - Estimate annual revenue

**Step 3: Verify Contact Info (2 hours for 20 prospects)**

Use Apollo.io or Hunter.io:

1. Go to Apollo.io
2. Enter company name
3. Search for decision maker
4. Verify email format
5. Check if email is bouncing/inactive
6. Note phone if available
7. Verify on LinkedIn

**Step 4: Research Company (2 hours for 20 prospects)**

For each prospect, research:

```
PROSPECT RESEARCH TEMPLATE

Company Name: TechSecure Inc
Website: www.techsecure.com
Industry: Cybersecurity
Size: 250 employees
Revenue: $20M (estimated)
Founded: 2015
Recent News: Raised $10M Series B funding
Growth: 60% YoY
Funding: Venture backed
Stock Exchange: Private
CEO: John Smith
Decision Maker: Sarah Johnson (CISO)
Email: sarah.johnson@techsecure.com
Phone: (415) 555-1234

Pain Points (from website):
- Need more security analysts
- Rapid growth straining team
- Looking for managed services

Fit Score: 85/100
Why Fit: Growing fast, well-funded, clear need
Next Action: Personalized email + LinkedIn
```

**Step 5: Create Fit Score (1 minute per prospect)**

Score based on:
- Company Fit (30%) - How well they match your ideal
- Marketing Need (25%) - Do they actually need what you offer?
- Budget (20%) - Can they afford it?
- Timing (15%) - Ready to buy now?
- Authority (10%) - Is this the right person?

```
Scoring Guide:

Company Fit:
- Perfect match (25-30): Same industry, size, stage
- Good fit (15-24): Similar to your best clients
- Potential (10-14): Could work with adjustments
- Unlikely (5-9): Some mismatches
- Wrong fit (0-4): Doesn't match profile

Marketing Need:
- Critical need (20-25): Desperate for help
- Clear need (15-19): Obvious problem
- Possible need (10-14): Might need help
- Unclear (5-9): Unsure if they need it
- No need (0-4): Don't need it

Budget:
- Very high budget (18-20): Can easily afford
- Good budget (14-17): Can afford premium
- Moderate (10-13): Can afford basic
- Limited (6-9): Tight budget
- No budget (0-5): Can't afford

Timing:
- Ready now (13-15): Budget approved, ready to buy
- Soon (10-12): Planning to buy in 1-3 months
- Consider (7-9): Will buy someday
- Unsure (4-6): Might not buy
- No timeline (0-3): Not in budget

Authority:
- Decision maker (9-10): Signs contracts
- Heavy influencer (7-8): Strong influence
- Influencer (5-6): Some influence
- Lower authority (3-4): Limited influence
- No authority (0-2): Just an observer

Example Scoring:
Company Fit: 25 (perfect match)
Marketing Need: 22 (clear need)
Budget: 16 (good budget)
Timing: 12 (soon)
Authority: 8 (strong influencer)
TOTAL: 83/100 = Hot Prospect
```

**Step 6: Save in Spreadsheet**

Columns needed:
```
Company Name | Decision Maker | Email | Phone | Industry | Size | Revenue 
| Fit Score | Status | Date Found | Notes | Next Action | Last Contact
```

## SECTION 2.2: MONITOR CHANNELS (Manual)

### Process: Manual Channel Monitoring

**Time Required:** 30 minutes per day = 2.5 hours per week

**Tools Needed:**
- Email (Gmail)
- LinkedIn
- Facebook Business
- Slack
- Spreadsheet

### Daily Monitoring Process:

**Morning (30 minutes):**

```
DAILY CHANNEL CHECK

8:00 AM - Email
- Check inbox for new leads
- Note sender, company, message intent
- Classify: Hot/Warm/Cold/Spam
- Save in spreadsheet

8:10 AM - LinkedIn Messages
- Check LinkedIn inbox
- Look for connection requests from targets
- Note introductions and inquiries
- Rate urgency

8:20 AM - Facebook/Instagram
- Check business messages
- Look for comments on posts
- Note engagement

8:30 AM - Slack
- Check team channel for leads
- Note any referrals
- Update team on findings
```

### Channel Monitoring Spreadsheet:

```
Columns:
- Date
- Channel (Email/LinkedIn/Facebook/etc)
- Sender Name
- Company
- Message Summary
- Classification (Hot/Warm/Cold/Spam)
- Urgency (Critical/High/Medium/Low)
- Assigned To
- Status (Responded/Pending/etc)
- Next Action
```

### Classification Guide:

```
HOT LEAD (Respond within 1 hour):
- Actively looking for your service
- Has budget
- Ready to discuss
- Clear pain point
- Decision maker
Example: "We're looking for a lead generation service ASAP"

WARM INQUIRY (Respond within 24 hours):
- Interested but not urgent
- Asking questions
- Exploring options
- Clear need but no timeline
Example: "We're interested in your services. Can you tell us more?"

COLD QUESTION (Respond within 3 days):
- Just asking general questions
- Not urgent
- Possible future interest
- No clear pain point yet
Example: "What's the difference between your service and [competitor]?"

SPAM (Archive):
- Unrelated to your service
- Automated message
- Not relevant
- Promotional emails from others
Example: "Buy this course" or "Check out my service"
```

## SECTION 2.4: COMPOSE MESSAGES (Manual)

### Process: Manual Message Writing

**Time Required:** 15-20 minutes per message

**Tools Needed:**
- Google Docs
- Grammarly
- Previous successful messages

### Message Writing Templates:

**TEMPLATE 1: LinkedIn Connection Request**

```
Hi [Name],

I came across your profile and [SPECIFIC COMPLIMENT about their company/role].

I'm [Your Title] at PMG Group, we specialize in lead generation for 
[their industry]. Given [SPECIFIC REASON they might need it], I thought 
it'd be worth connecting.

Would love to connect and maybe grab coffee virtually?

[Your Name]
[Your Title]
[Your LinkedIn]

TIPS:
- Keep under 300 characters
- Personalize the compliment
- Show you know their company
- Don't ask for anything yet
- Use casual tone on LinkedIn
```

**TEMPLATE 2: Follow-up Email After LinkedIn Connection**

```
Subject: Quick idea for [Company Name] - [Personalized mention]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for connecting on LinkedIn. I noticed [SPECIFIC OBSERVATION about 
their business] and it got me thinking...

Companies in your space are typically struggling with [SPECIFIC PAIN POINT].
We've helped [SIMILAR COMPANY] [RESULT ACHIEVED] by [YOUR APPROACH].

No pressure at all, but would [DATE] or [DATE] work for a quick 15-min 
call to explore if we could do something similar?

Happy to grab coffee virtually 😊

[Your Name]
[Your Title]
[Your Company]
[Your Phone]

TIPS:
- Keep it short (under 200 words)
- One specific pain point
- One proof (case study/client)
- One easy ask (15 minutes)
- Use casual friendly tone
- Add personality (emoji is okay)
```

**TEMPLATE 3: Cold Email (No Prior Contact)**

```
Subject: [Company Name]'s Q3 growth opportunity

Hi [Name],

Quick context: I help [your target] generate [X] qualified leads per month. 
We specialize in [your niche].

I was researching [Company Name] and noticed:
- [Specific observation 1]
- [Specific observation 2]
- [Specific observation 3]

These are the exact issues we've solved for [similar company], where we 
generated [X] leads in [timeframe] at [cost].

Not saying it's a fit, but would be worth a quick conversation.

Open Thursday 2pm or Friday 10am? 

[Your Name]
[Your Title]
[Your Company]
[Phone]
[LinkedIn URL]

TIPS:
- Personalized subject line
- Specific observations (not generic)
- Proof (one case study)
- Specific time options
- Not pushy
- Professional but personable
```

**TEMPLATE 4: Objection Response - "Already Have a Vendor"**

```
Subject: Re: [Their subject] - Quick thought

Hi [Name],

Totally get it - you're already working with [their vendor]. That makes sense.

Here's the thing though: most companies we've talked to started with another 
vendor too. What we usually find is [COMMON PROBLEM with current vendors]:

- Quality issue? [Our solution]
- Cost issue? [Our solution]
- Relationship issue? [Our solution]

No pressure at all. But if you're open to it, I could do a quick audit of 
what you're getting and show you a comparison.

Would that be interesting?

[Your Name]

TIPS:
- Acknowledge their situation
- Identify common problem with competitors
- Offer no-risk audit
- Easy decision (just a conversation)
```

**TEMPLATE 5: Follow-up After No Response**

```
Subject: Just checking in re: [Original topic]

Hi [Name],

Haven't heard back from my last email - no problem! 

I know you're busy. Just wanted to make sure it didn't get lost in your inbox.

That idea about [specific benefit] is still valid if you want to explore it.

No pressure - just let me know if you want to chat.

[Your Name]

TIPS:
- Casual tone
- Don't be pushy
- Remind of value
- Give them easy out
- Use if: 5-7 days have passed
```

**TEMPLATE 6: Close Follow-up (Last Attempt)**

```
Subject: Final check-in - [Company Name]

Hi [Name],

This will be my last email - promise! 😊

I've reached out a few times about helping [Company Name] with lead generation.
Clearly this might not be the right time/fit.

If you ever want to revisit this, I'm here. No pressure.

Either way, best of luck with [Company Name]'s growth.

[Your Name]

TIPS:
- This is the last touch
- Be graceful about it
- Leave door open
- Professional but friendly
- Don't expect response
- Use after: 2 weeks with no response
```

- Day 0: Initial contact

   - Send message
   - Update next action date
   - Set calendar reminder

1. Check calendar for follow-ups due today
2. Execute follow-ups

- Create custom proposal
- Present tailored solution
- Address objections
- Get feedback
Exit Criteria:
- They see value

Activities:
- Discuss pricing
- Work out terms
- Answer questions
- Build relationship
Exit Criteria:
- Price agreed
- Terms agreed
- Ready to sign
Next Stage: Close

STAGE 4: CLOSING (Typical: 1-3 days)
Status: Getting signature

- Send contract
- Follow up on signature
- Answer final questions
- Coordinate with legal
Exit Criteria:
- Contract signed
- Payment received

Status: Contract signed, payment received
Goal: Document success, move to delivery
Activities:
- Close opportunity
- Document success
- Celebrate
- Transition to service team

STAGE 5: LOST (Final)
Status: They chose competitor or said no
Goal: Learn and move on
Activities:
- Understand why they didn't buy

- Document lesson learned
- Keep relationship warm
