1. DASHBOARD
The Dashboard is your command center, the first page you see after login. It provides a high-level overview of your entire business: key metrics, quick actions, pipeline status, recent leads, and system health.

1.1 — KPI cards display

HOW IT WORKS:
The main area shows metric cards: Active Leads (total count of current leads), Pipeline Value (total dollar value of deals in progress), Conversion Rate (percentage of leads that become clients), Revenue (MRR or total). These numbers pull from the actual database, so they reflect your real data.

IN AI AUTONOMOUS MODE:
All KPIs auto-update as AI agents score leads, advance deals, and process data. Numbers change in real-time.

IN HYBRID MODE:
KPIs update, but some items may be "pending your review" before counting toward metrics.

IN HUMAN CONTROLLED MODE:
KPIs only update when you manually process items. Numbers reflect your manual work only.

EXPECTED RESULT:
PASS if cards show with numbers (even if zero on a fresh system). PARTIAL if cards show but numbers seem wrong. FAIL if cards are missing or showing error messages.

Shows all deals including those auto-advanced by AI.

IN HYBRID MODE:
Shows all deals with review badges on AI-processed ones.
