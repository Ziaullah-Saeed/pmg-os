PART 16 — PRODUCTION GOVERNANCE & APPROVAL LAW
All generated assets must follow a controlled lifecycle. No raw generation may bypass review and move directly into final or public use. Production without governance introduces reputational risk, quality instability, and traceability failure. 
The mandatory lifecycle shall be:
Generate → Preview → Review → Revise / Regenerate → Approve → Finalize
This governance model must apply to:
•	brand outputs 
•	websites 
•	pages 
•	proposals 
•	decks 
•	scripts 
•	videos 
•	content 
•	sales materials 
•	client-facing deliverables 
•	onboarding assets 
•	presentation materials 
The system must prohibit:
•	uncontrolled finalization 
•	direct publishing from raw generation 
•	bypassing the preview stage 
•	invisible revisions 
•	weak version control 
•	approval ambiguity 
The production layer must maintain a full history of:
•	drafts 
•	revisions 
•	review notes 
•	rejection reasons 
•	approvals 
•	final versions 
•	timestamps 
•	actors involved in review or approval 
•	rationale for acceptance or rejection 
Governance in production is non-negotiable. The purpose is not bureaucracy for its own sake. The purpose is quality, accountability, and confidence in what leaves the system.
